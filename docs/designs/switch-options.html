View switch: three options

Each option shown in the desktop header and on a 375px phone. The current switch is the "Simple | Code" segmented control.

A. "Dev mode" toggle (recommended)

A small on/off switch with a label, like a settings toggle. It says plainly that this is a mode you can turn on. It's compact enough that your name stays in the phone header. Turned on, the track goes dark and the page becomes the editor. The same toggle sits in the editor's title bar, turned on, so turning it off brings you back.

B. Floating "View as code" button

A dark pill fixed to the bottom-right corner, using the editor's colours. The header stays clean, and on phones it sits where your thumb is. It's the most eye-catching option. The risk is that people sometimes take a floating corner button for a chat widget, and it can cover content as you scroll.

C. Quiet link in the nav

Reads like one more menu item, set apart by a thin divider, with a green </>. The lowest-key option: it never competes with your content, but some visitors won't notice it. On phones it shrinks to a small </> button.
