Header options, with the floating "View as code" button

The Dev mode toggle leaves the header and becomes a dark pill fixed to the bottom-right corner (a </> chip plus "View as code"; on phones it keeps a short "Code" label so it doesn't read as a chat bubble). In the editor the same button turns light and says "Simple view". Each header option below is shown at the top of the page, desktop and 375px.

A. Floating pill header (recommended)

The header detaches from the edges and becomes a rounded, frosted bar. The current section gets a soft highlight. With the toggle gone, the right side gets one clear action, "Email me", so a recruiter can act from anywhere on the page. It's the most current-looking of the three, and the Dribbble and Awwwards references lean this way.

C. Editorial masthead

Like the top of a magazine: links on the left, your name in the serif italic in the centre, "Get in touch" on the right, with a firm rule underneath. It goes furthest with the editorial direction and is the most distinctive. The tradeoff is that the name competes with the big hero headline right below it.
